How the booking assistant works

It is narrow on purpose. The assistant never decides what "available" means and it cannot write to your calendar with free text.

  1. Someone texts your business number. If we do not recognise the number, they are saved as a new lead before anything else happens.
  2. The server works out your genuinely open slots for the next seven days from your working hours and the visits already booked.
  3. The assistant is handed that list and may only offer times from it. It offers one or two, never a wall of options.
  4. Nothing is booked until the customer confirms a specific time, and the booking happens through a structured function call that is re-checked against your calendar as it is written.
  5. If the conversation goes about four turns without getting anywhere, or touches pricing, or the customer asks for a person, it stops and flags the thread for you.
